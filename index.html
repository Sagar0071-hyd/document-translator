📄 Document Translator

Translates your document in place — the layout, formatting, tables, graphics, and images stay exactly the same, only the text changes. Word stays Word, Excel stays Excel, text stays text, PDF stays PDF, images stay images. PDFs are translated from their real text — layout, fonts and pictures kept, images left as-is. Photos and scanned images are read with OCR and translated in place.
